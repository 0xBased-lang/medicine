## 🎯 PLATFORM SPECIFICATIONS

### TikTok
- **Format:** Vertical (9:16 ratio, 1080x1920px)
- **Length:** 15-60 seconds (sweet spot: 45 seconds)
- **File Size:** Max 287.6 MB
- **Captions:** ESSENTIAL (80% watch without sound)
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant tags
- **Best Times:** 6-10 AM, 7-11 PM

### Instagram Reels
- **Format:** Vertical (9:16 ratio, 1080x1920px)
- **Length:** 15-90 seconds (sweet spot: 60 seconds)
- **File Size:** Max 4 GB
- **Captions:** Highly recommended
- **Hashtags:** Up to 30 (use 10-15)
- **Best Times:** 11 AM-1 PM, 7-9 PM

### YouTube Shorts
- **Format:** Vertical (9:16 ratio, 1080x1920px)
- **Length:** Up to 60 seconds
- **File Size:** Max 256 GB (realistically keep under 100 MB)
- **Captions:** Built-in CC recommended
- **Hashtags:** Include #Shorts in title/description
- **Best Times:** 12-3 PM, 7-10 PM

## 🚀 POSTING WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Prepare Your Video
- [ ] Video is vertical (9:16)
- [ ] Duration: 45-60 seconds
- [ ] Captions are clear and readable
- [ ] Audio levels are balanced
- [ ] Hook in first 3 seconds

### Step 2: TikTok Upload
1. Open TikTok app
2. Tap "+" to create
3. Tap "Upload" (bottom right)
4. Select your video
5. Add caption (see templates above)
6. Add hashtags (3-5 tags)
7. Enable captions (if not already added)
8. Select cover (pick engaging frame)
9. Post!

### Step 3: Instagram Reel Upload
1. Open Instagram app
2. Tap "+" → "Reel"
3. Upload your video
4. Add audio (optional trending sound)
5. Add stickers/text (optional)
6. Write caption with hashtags
7. Select cover image
8. Share to Feed + Stories
9. Post!

### Step 4: YouTube Shorts Upload
1. Open YouTube app
2. Tap "+" → "Create a Short"
3. Upload your video
4. Add title (include #Shorts)
5. Write description with hashtags
6. Select visibility (Public)
7. Choose thumbnail
8. Upload!

## 💡 ENGAGEMENT TIPS

### Hook Viewers in First 3 Seconds
- ✅ Start with question: "Did you know..."
- ✅ Bold statement: "Ancient healers knew..."
- ✅ Visual hook: Show herbs/books immediately
- ❌ Avoid: Long intros or explanations

### Increase Watch Time
- ✅ Pattern interrupt (change visual every 3-5 sec)
- ✅ Text overlay for key points
- ✅ Background music (not too loud)
- ✅ End with question or CTA

### Boost Comments
- Ask questions in caption
- Reply to every comment
- Create controversy (gently!)
- Share personal experience

### Encourage Shares
- "Tag someone who needs this!"
- "Send this to your wellness friend"
- Valuable, practical information
- Clear, memorable takeaways

## 📈 ANALYTICS TO TRACK

### TikTok Analytics
- **Watch Time:** Aim for >50% average
- **Likes:** Good engagement = 5-10% of views
- **Comments:** Engage back within 1 hour
- **Shares:** Most valuable metric!
- **Profile Visits:** Indicates interest in you

### Instagram Insights
- **Reach:** How many unique accounts
- **Interactions:** Likes + Comments + Saves
- **Saves:** VERY valuable (people want to revisit)
- **Shares:** Indicates viral potential
- **Profile Visits:** Growth indicator

### YouTube Analytics  
- **Watch Time:** Aim for >80%
- **CTR:** Click-through rate from browse/search
- **Subscribers:** Gained from this Short
- **Likes:** Should be 5-15% of views
- **Comments:** Engage immediately
