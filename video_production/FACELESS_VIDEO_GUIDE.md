## 🚀 QUICK START: Style 1 (Stock Footage)

### Step 1: Download Stock Footage (5 minutes)

**Visit Pexels.com and search for:**
- "medicinal herbs"
- "chamomile flower"
- "echinacea"
- "valerian root"
- "ancient books"
- "herbal medicine"
- "natural remedies"
- "botanical garden"

**Download Tips:**
- Get 5-8 short clips (5-10 seconds each)
- Choose vertical (9:16) when available
- Select high quality (1080p minimum)
- Mix close-ups with wide shots

**Alternative Sources:**
- Pixabay.com
- Coverr.co
- Mixkit.co
- Videvo.net

---

### Step 2: Open CapCut (Mobile or Desktop)

**Mobile App (Recommended for beginners):**
1. Download CapCut from App Store/Google Play
2. Open app → Tap "New Project"
3. Import your downloaded footage
4. Import voiceover_script1.mp3

**Desktop (More control):**
1. Download from capcut.com
2. Create new project
3. Set aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
4. Import media files

---

### Step 3: Basic Timeline Setup (5 minutes)

```
Timeline Layout:

Track 1 (Video):  [Clip1][Clip2][Clip3][Clip4][Clip5]
Track 2 (Audio):  [========Voiceover========]
Track 3 (Music):  [=====Background Music=====]
Track 4 (Text):   [Caption1][Caption2][Caption3]
```

**Instructions:**
1. Drag first video clip to timeline
2. Drag voiceover to audio track
3. Trim video clips to match voiceover length (~40 seconds)
4. Change clips every 3-5 seconds for variety

---

### Step 4: Add Auto Captions (2 minutes)

**In CapCut:**
1. Tap "Text" → "Auto Captions"
2. Select language: English
3. Wait 30 seconds for generation
4. Review and fix any errors

**Caption Style Tips:**
- Font: Bold, easy to read (Montserrat, Arial Black)
- Color: White text, black background/outline
- Size: Large (readable on phone)
- Position: Center or bottom third
- Animation: Pop in/out

---

### Step 5: Add Background Music (2 minutes)

**Music Selection:**
- Low volume (10-15% of voiceover)
- No lyrics (instrumental only)
- Calming/inspiring mood
- Royalty-free

**Free Sources:**
- CapCut built-in library
- YouTube Audio Library
- Pixabay Music
- FreePD.com

**How to Add:**
1. Search "ambient" or "inspiring" in CapCut
2. Preview and select track
3. Add to timeline
4. Reduce volume to 10-15%
5. Fade in/out at beginning/end

---

### Step 6: Enhance with Effects (3 minutes)

**Recommended Effects:**

**Transitions:**
- Between clips: Dissolve or Blur
- Keep it subtle (0.3-0.5 seconds)
- Don't overuse

**Text Effects:**
- Highlight medical terms in different color
- Add emoji for key points 🌿💪✨
- Pop animation for emphasis

**Color Grading:**
- Slight warmth (+5-10)
- Increased saturation for herbs (+10-15)
- Keep it natural

---

### Step 7: Export Settings (2 minutes)

**Optimal Settings:**
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Quality:** High or Smart HDR
- **Format:** MP4
- **File Size:** Aim for under 50MB

**Export:**
1. Tap "Export" (top right)
2. Select settings above
3. Wait for processing (1-2 minutes)
4. Save to camera roll

## 📱 CAPCUT MOBILE TUTORIAL (Step-by-Step)

### Creating Your First Faceless Video

**Total Time: 15 minutes**

1. **Import Media (2 min)**
   - Tap "+" → New Project
   - Select 5-8 herb videos
   - Tap "Add"
   - Import voiceover_script1.mp3

2. **Arrange Clips (3 min)**
   - Clips appear on timeline
   - Tap clip → Trim to 3-5 seconds each
   - Total length should match voiceover (~40s)
   - Rearrange by long-pressing and dragging

3. **Add Voiceover (1 min)**
   - Tap "Audio" → "Sounds"
   - Find and select voiceover_script1.mp3
   - It appears on audio track
   - Ensure it starts at beginning

4. **Auto Captions (2 min)**
   - Tap "Text" at bottom
   - Tap "Auto Captions"
   - Select "English"
   - Wait 30 seconds
   - Review and fix typos

5. **Style Captions (2 min)**
   - Tap caption on timeline
   - Select all captions
   - Choose style: "Bold" or "Modern"
   - Adjust size: 70-80%
   - Change color: White with black outline

6. **Add Music (2 min)**
   - Tap "Audio" → "Sounds"
   - Search "ambient meditation"
   - Select calming track
   - Reduce volume to 15%
   - Tap "Fade" → Fade in/out

7. **Final Touches (2 min)**
   - Tap between clips → Add "Dissolve" transition
   - Tap "Adjust" → Increase saturation +10
   - Preview full video
   - Make any adjustments

8. **Export (1 min)**
   - Tap "Export" (top right)
   - Resolution: 1080p
   - Frame rate: 30fps
   - Quality: High
   - Export and save!

## 🎯 FACELESS CONTENT BEST PRACTICES

### Hook Viewers (First 3 Seconds)

**Visual Hooks:**
- Striking imagery (bright herbs, old books)
- Bold text: "Ancient Healing Secrets"
- Pattern interrupt (quick zoom/transition)

**Audio Hook:**
- Start voiceover immediately
- No intro music/delays
- First sentence is the hook

---

### Maintain Engagement (Middle)

**Every 3-5 seconds:**
- Change video clip
- Add text overlay
- Slight zoom or pan
- Visual variety

**Caption Strategy:**
- Highlight key medical terms
- Use emoji for emphasis 🌿💪✨
- Different colors for categories

---

### Strong Ending (Last 5 Seconds)

**Call to Action:**
- "Follow for more ancient remedies"
- "Comment your favorite herb 👇"
- "Share with a wellness friend"

**Visual:**
- Show all herbs together
- End on compelling image
- Final text: CTA

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR FACELESS SUCCESS

### 1. Consistent Visual Style
- Choose 2-3 color themes
- Stick to same font styles
- Maintain brand feel

### 2. Quality Over Quantity
- 1 great video > 5 mediocre ones
- Invest time in clip selection
- Perfect your caption timing

### 3. Optimize for Silent Viewing
- 80% watch without sound
- Make captions tell the story
- Visual storytelling is key

### 4. Batch Production
- Edit 3-5 videos in one session
- Use same template/style
- Save time with efficiency

### 5. Test Different Styles
- Week 1: Stock footage
- Week 2: Text overlay focus
- Week 3: Historical illustrations
- Week 4: Mix of all three
