# 🎥 Video Production Guide - 3 Easy Methods

Choose the method that works best for you!

## METHOD 1: Record Yourself (Most Personal & Engaging)

### ✅ Best For:
- Building personal brand
- Maximum audience connection
- Establishing authority

### 📱 What You Need:
- Smartphone camera
- Good lighting (natural or ring light)
- Quiet room
- Your script (printed or on second device)

### 🎬 Step-by-Step:

1. **Setup (5 minutes)**
   - Clean background (plain wall or bookshelf)
   - Position camera at eye level
   - Test lighting (face should be well-lit)
   - Enable grid lines on camera

2. **Recording (10 minutes per video)**
   - Read hook with enthusiasm (record 3 takes)
   - Read full script naturally (2-3 takes)
   - Speak slower than normal (important!)
   - Make eye contact with camera
   - Use hand gestures for emphasis

3. **Quick Tips**
   - Smile when you start
   - Pause between sentences
   - Don't worry about perfection
   - Energy > Production quality

### 📲 Recommended Apps:
- **iPhone:** Built-in Camera + iMovie
- **Android:** Camera + CapCut
- **Desktop:** OBS Studio (free)

## METHOD 2: Text-to-Speech (Fastest & Easiest)

### ✅ Best For:
- Quick content creation
- Faceless channel
- Testing content before investing time

### 🔧 Free TTS Options:

**Option A: ElevenLabs (Best Quality)**
- Website: elevenlabs.io
- Free tier: 10,000 characters/month
- Natural voices
- High quality

**Option B: Google Cloud TTS (Good Quality)**
- Free tier available
- Multiple languages
- Wavenet voices

**Option C: macOS Built-in (Quick & Easy)**
```bash
# Generate voiceover from your script
say -v Samantha -o ~/medicine/video_production/voiceover.aiff "Your script here"

# Convert to MP3
ffmpeg -i ~/medicine/video_production/voiceover.aiff ~/medicine/video_production/voiceover.mp3
```

### 🎬 Workflow:

1. **Generate Voice (2 minutes)**
   - Copy script to TTS service
   - Select natural-sounding voice
   - Download MP3 file

2. **Create Video (5 minutes)**
   - Open CapCut or iMovie
   - Import background footage (Pexels, Unsplash)
   - Add voiceover
   - Add text overlays for key points

3. **Polish (3 minutes)**
   - Add captions (auto-generated)
   - Background music (low volume)
   - Transitions between scenes

### 🎵 Free Resources:
- **Stock Footage:** Pexels.com, Pixabay.com
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound, YouTube Audio Library
- **Editing:** CapCut (free, mobile + desktop)

## METHOD 3: Hybrid (Voice + Text Overlay)

### ✅ Best For:
- Maximum accessibility
- Viewers watching without sound
- Professional look

### 🎬 Workflow:

1. **Record or Generate Voice**
   - Use Method 1 or Method 2 above

2. **Add Dynamic Captions**
   - CapCut auto-captions (highly recommended!)
   - Style: Bold white text, black background
   - Highlight key words in different color

3. **Visual Layers**
   - Background: Stock footage or images
   - Middle: Captions (auto-synced)
   - Top: Your face (optional, picture-in-picture)

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- 80% of viewers watch without sound
- Captions increase watch time by 40%
- Highlight medical terms in different colors

## 🎨 Video Editing Quick Start

### CapCut (Recommended - Free & Easy)

**Download:**
- Mobile: iOS App Store / Google Play
- Desktop: capcut.com

**Basic Editing (10 minutes):**

1. Create New Project
2. Import Assets:
   - Voiceover/recording
   - Background footage
   - Images (optional)

3. Timeline Assembly:
   - Drag background to timeline
   - Add voiceover on audio track
   - Trim to match audio length

4. Add Captions:
   - Tap "Text" → "Auto Captions"
   - Select language (English)
   - Wait 30 seconds for generation
   - Customize style

5. Enhancements:
   - Background music (10-15% volume)
   - Transitions (subtle!)
   - Effects (minimal)

6. Export:
   - Resolution: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
   - Frame rate: 30fps
   - Quality: High
   - Format: MP4
